Blokmix · app icon round 5 · Oct 23, 2026

Icon round 5

Inspired by Matthew Skiles’ approach: one tactile hero object with real light, depth and material on a lit tile. These are original Blokmix objects, not copies of his icons. The flat canvas moments from round 4 are saved as a set of custom product icons for later.

00 · Rendered in Blender

The grab and Pinboard, built in 3D

Real geometry, materials and light instead of drawn gradients. The slot is carved into the canvas, the block and card are glossy coated plastic, and the shadows come from a single key light at the top left. The dark versions now use a flat, matte plum-ink tile with no washed-out gradient.

At 16 and 32 px a render turns soft, so whichever you pick gets a flat drawn favicon built from the same shapes. On a dark home screen the dark tiles’ edges nearly disappear, which is normal for dark app icons. If you want more separation I can add a faint rim light around the tile.

03 · Shortlist

The three I’d take forward

Each next to the wordmark.

The grab

My first pick. It is the brand idea in one object (a block, lifted from a canvas), and the glossy pink block echoes the block in the wordmark. The slot behind it gives the story a before and after.

Zip

The most unusual, and the most tactile. No other design tool uses a zipper, and it says “two sources become one” without words. It is busier at 16 px, so the favicon would be just the pull on a split tile.

Pinboard

The clearest about what gets sampled: website sections. Good for an audience who wants to know what the tool does, but quieter as a symbol.

These are hand-drawn SVG with gradients and blur. Once you pick one, the final master gets a production pass: refined lighting, a flat fallback for 16 and 32 px, and PNG exports at every App Store and macOS size.

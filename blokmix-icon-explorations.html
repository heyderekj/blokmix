03 · Shortlist

The three I’d take forward

Each one shown in a lockup with the wordmark, since the icon and wordmark have to look like they belong together.

Block b

The tightest fit with the wordmark, since it is literally the wordmark’s first letter and block. It is also the most ownable at 16 px. Its weakness is the mix story, which the motion would carry: the block slides into the b.

Splice

The best balance of block and mix: one block made of two sources, and the offset makes it feel cut on the beat. It also animates well, with the halves sliding into line.

Crossfader

The most hip-hop of the eight. Any DJ will recognize it at a glance. It is a little busier at 16 px, so the favicon would drop the track and keep the three blocks.

Ratings are my read of how clearly each frame says “block”, how clearly it says “mix”, and how well it holds up at 16 px. Pick one (or a pair to combine) and I’ll rebuild it as final masters: pixel-snapped favicons, lockups and PNG exports, replacing the 2×2 everywhere.

03 · Shortlist

The three I’d take forward

Each one next to the wordmark.

Drag

The most ownable action. The cursor tells people at once that this is a design tool, and the empty slot behind the block tells them the block came from somewhere. It animates well too: the block lifts out and follows the cursor.

Snap

The cleverest. Pink smart guides are already how design tools show alignment, so the brand color does real work. It shows two sources being mixed into one layout. At 16 px the guides drop out and it becomes two blocks over a section.

Group

The simplest way to say mix on a canvas: two sources, one selection. Bold at every size, and the overlapping blocks keep a little of the square-o shadow wordmark’s offset energy.

Ratings are my read of how clearly each concept says “block”, how clearly it says “mix”, and how well it holds at 16 px. Pick one or combine two, for example Group with Snap’s pink guides, and I’ll build it as final masters and replace the 2×2 everywhere.

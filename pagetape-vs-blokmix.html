Pagetape vs Blokmix

Two names for the tool that lets web designers sample sections from any site and remix them into a new page. Each side below covers what the name says, how it looks, and what you can own today.

Decision: Blokmix.

It has more of a hip-hop feel. This page stays as the record of the comparison, and the full brand guide builds on Side B.

Side Apage + mixtape
pagetape
Say it
PAGE-tape. Rhymes with mixtape.
Use it
“Let’s pagetape a homepage from Stripe’s hero and Linear’s pricing.”
Taglines
  • Mixtapes for the web.
  • Every great site starts as a mixtape.
  • Tape your next homepage together.

For

  • Reads as web pages plus mixtape on first sight.
  • Keeps the sound of the project’s working name.
  • Every domain checked is open, bare .com included.
  • “Tape” is also the action: taping sections into a page.

Against

  • No K, Z or X, so it’s less distinctive on the page.
  • @pagetape is blocked on X. Use @pagetapehq.
  • Two real words joined. The wordmark has to make it one name.
Side Bblock + mix
blokmix
Say it
BLOCK-mix.
Use it
“Blokmix the footer from Vercel into this page.”
Taglines
  • Mix blocks from any site.
  • Sample the web, block by block.
  • Your next site, built from the best of the web.

For

  • K matches Koati. X is a high-value Scrabble letter.
  • Blocks are the unit every site builder uses.
  • Every domain checked is open, and @blokmix on X is free.
  • Short and punchy as a verb.

Against

  • Heard aloud, people type “blockmix”, and blockmix.com belongs to someone else.
  • Storyblok, a CMS, already uses the “blok” spelling.
  • Harder and cooler in tone. The pink has to warm it up.
  • @blokmix on Instagram is taken. Use @blokmixhq.

Logo, icon and wordmark

First sketches

Each mark is shown on paper and on ink, with the app icon at 120, 64 and 16 px. The 16 px version is simplified so it still reads as a browser favicon.

Pagetape tape over sections

IdeaSections from different sites, taped into one page. The torn pink strip is the brand device and can be reused for highlights, empty states and loading.

TypeRounded geometric sans, lowercase, tight spacing. “tape” always sits on the pink strip with ink text (7:1 contrast).

AvoidCassette clip art, Scotch or duct-tape cues, paper textures.

Blokmix the block mid-drop

IdeaBlocks from different sites snapping into one grid. The pink block is the one you just sampled, and its dashed slot shows where it lands.

TypeExtra-wide, extra-bold grotesque, lowercase, tight spacing. The “o” is a solid pink square, the same block as the icon.

AvoidLEGO or toy-brick cues, Storyblok’s teal, rainbow blocks.

Shared color system

Pink, with a job for each shade

Where this lands

Next steps

Lean: Pagetape.

It’s the clearest web-plus-mixtape name, and it has the cleanest availability, including a typo-catcher domain. Choose Blokmix if the K and X punch matters more than the blockmix.com risk.

If Pagetape

  1. Register pagetape.com, pagetape.fm and pagetapes.com.
  2. Claim @pagetapehq on X and @pagetape on Instagram.
  3. Have a trademark attorney run a full clearance before you file.

If Blokmix

  1. Register blokmix.com and blokmix.fm.
  2. Claim @blokmix on X and @blokmixhq on Instagram.
  3. Have a trademark attorney run a full clearance before you file.

How this was checked, Oct 23, 2026. Domains: registry WHOIS and RDAP lookups. X: X’s sign-up availability check. Instagram: profile pages loaded while signed out. “No active profile” usually means the handle is free but can also be a deactivated account, so claim it in the app to be sure. Trademarks: an exact-word search of the USPTO trademark database. This is a quick screen, not legal clearance.

Nearest-name sources: Pagetify on Shopify, Devcon DFense Blok mixer.

What changes in the first month

The same projects and the same people, with far less of the friction that used to fill the week.

Before After
  • Status lives in five different inboxes
    One board everyone checks in the morning
  • Files renamed final, final-2, final-real
    Every version kept, the latest always on top
  • Feedback arrives as screenshots in chat
    Notes pinned right where the change belongs
  • Weekly meetings just to catch up
    Progress you can read in two minutes, any time
  • Clients wonder what’s happening
    Clients see the plan and approve in one click
  • Handover is a scramble at the end
    A tidy recap is ready the day you finish

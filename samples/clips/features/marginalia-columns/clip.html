The reading room

Six habits of an unhurried reader

Marginalia was built on a simple premise: that reading well is slow work, and the tools around it should keep their voices low and their margins wide.

  1. IChapter

    Write in the margin

    Annotate any page on the web in ink that stays exactly where you left it.

  2. IIChapter

    Keep a commonplace book

    Every highlight gathers into one quiet volume, arranged by theme rather than by date.

  3. IIIChapter

    Follow the footnotes

    Citations open beside the text, so a tangent never costs you your place.

  4. IVChapter

    Read by lamplight

    Warm paper by day and a dimmed study by night, with type set for long sittings.

  5. VChapter

    Lend a book

    Share an annotated essay with a friend, marginal notes and all.

  6. VIChapter

    Return in a season

    Gentle reminders resurface old passages when you’re ready to read them again.

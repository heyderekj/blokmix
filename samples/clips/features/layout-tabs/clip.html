The process

Four steps from first call to launch day

Every project follows the same calm rhythm, so you always know what happens next and what I need from you.

  • An unhurried call about your work, your clients and what the site has to do. You get a short written plan the same week.

  • Two directions for your home page, then one we refine together until it feels unmistakably yours.

  • The approved design becomes a fast, accessible site on a private link you can click through any time.

  • We go live together, I walk you through editing, and I stay close for the first month of real visitors.

Launch checklist

The details that are easy to forget

Before any site goes live, it’s checked against the same list. You never have to think about it, and nothing slips through.

  • Quick on any connection

    Images sized and compressed, pages tested on a slow phone network.

  • Readable by everyone

    Contrast, keyboard use and screen readers checked on every page.

  • Found in search

    Clear titles, descriptions and a sitemap submitted on launch day.

  • Looks right when shared

    Share images and previews for every page, not just the home page.

  • Forms that arrive

    Every enquiry form tested end to end, straight to your inbox.

  • Backed up and secure

    Secure connection, daily backups and a simple way to restore.

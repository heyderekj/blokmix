How I work

Small studio habits, big studio care

A few promises that hold for every project, whether it’s a one-page portfolio or a full shop.

  • You talk to me

    No account managers or hand-offs. The person you brief is the person doing the work.

  • Dates that hold

    A clear plan on day one, weekly check-ins and a launch date we both believe in.

  • One fixed price

    You know the full cost before we start, with no hourly surprises at the end.

  • Care after launch

    Thirty days of fixes and tweaks included, then simple monthly care if you want it.

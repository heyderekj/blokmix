How it works

A quieter way to run every project, told in four chapters

Read down the page. The picture stays beside you while each step explains what changes for you and your clients.

  1. 01

    Bring everything in

    Forward the emails, drop in the files and paste the notes from your last call. Nothing needs to be tidy yet; that’s the next step’s job.

  2. 02

    Shape the plan

    Loose pieces become a short list of milestones, each with an owner and a date you agreed on together.

  3. 03

    Work in the open

    Drafts, questions and approvals happen in one place your client can see, so status meetings quietly disappear.

  4. 04

    Wrap up cleanly

    Hand over the final files with a short recap of what was decided and why, ready for whoever picks it up next.

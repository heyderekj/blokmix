The process

Three steps from first call to launch day

Most small sites don’t need a six-month process. They need someone who listens, decides and ships.

  1. 01

    A long first call about your work, your clients and what the site has to do, followed by a short written plan with a fixed price.

  2. 02

    Words first, then design in the browser. You see real pages within a fortnight and comment on them where they live.

  3. 03

    Launch, a handover video and a month of fixes on me. After that, I’m an email away whenever you need a hand.

Most projects take six to eight weeks.

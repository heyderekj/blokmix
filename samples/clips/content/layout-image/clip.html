Behind the work

A studio the size of a kitchen table

Everything here is made in one room, by one person, with a lot of coffee and very few meetings.

We start with a long conversation about your work, your clients and what the site has to do. I write a first draft of the words before anything is designed, because the words decide the layout.

Then I design in the browser, so you see real pages early and we can change course while it’s still cheap to. You get a link to the work in progress, not a deck of mock-ups.

Launch is a handover, not a goodbye. You get a site you can edit yourself, a short video on how, and a month of fixes on me.

Two minutes on how a mix becomes a site

A short walk through the whole loop, from the first clip to the packet an agent builds from.

Most sites start as a pile of references. The trouble is that a pile doesn’t tell you what to build first, or how the pieces should sit together.

So we treat every reference as a section you can keep, move or swap, and let one style guide pull them into the same voice.

Taste is a collection. The trick is turning it into a starting point.

When the page reads right, the packet carries every decision forward, so the build starts where the thinking stopped.

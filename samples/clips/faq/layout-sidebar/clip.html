What do I get when I sign up?

Access to every feature in your plan, ready to use straight away. There’s nothing to install.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. The free plan never expires, so you can take your time and upgrade only when you need more.

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades apply right away and downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period.

Do you offer discounts for teams?

Yearly billing saves about 20%, and larger teams can talk to us about volume pricing.

Is my data secure?

Your work is encrypted in transit and at rest, and you can export or delete it whenever you like.

How do I get help?

Browse the docs, ask the community, or reach our support team directly from your account.

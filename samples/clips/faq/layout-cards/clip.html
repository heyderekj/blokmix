FAQ

Answers, all in one place

Short and honest answers to what clients ask before we work together.

  • How soon can we start?

    I usually book four to six weeks ahead. Once we’ve agreed the scope, a small deposit holds your spot.

  • What do you need from me?

    Your goals, any brand assets you have, and an hour a week for feedback. I’ll guide you through the rest.

  • Do you write the copy too?

    I can shape it with you, or bring in a writer I trust. Either way, words come before layouts.

  • Can I update the site myself?

    Yes. You get a simple editor and a short video walk-through, so small changes never wait on me.

  • What if I need changes later?

    Most clients move onto a care plan, but you can also book a few hours whenever something comes up.

  • How do payments work?

    Half up front and half at launch, by bank transfer or card. Larger projects can split into stages.

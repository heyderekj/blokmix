Our story

Printed slowly, on purpose

We publish four books a year, each set by hand and bound to last a lifetime of rereading.

Folio began in a garage with a borrowed press and a stubborn idea: that the things we read deserve the same care as the things we eat.

Today we're a team of six, still in a small workshop, still choosing every paper by touch.

“A book is a promise that someone thought this was worth the trees.”

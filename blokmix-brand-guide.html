A1

Blokmix. One word, a K, no C.

Say it BLOCK-mix. Write it with a capital B in sentences. The logo is the only place it appears in lowercase. The K is the point: it marks the name as ours and ties it to Koati.

Write it like this

  • BlokmixIn sentences, headlines and UI.
  • BLOKMIXOnly in all-caps display headlines, like the flyer.
  • Blokmix that footer.As a verb in talk and marketing copy. Keep product buttons literal, like “Clip a section”.

Not like this

  • BlockmixThe C spelling belongs to someone else’s domain.
  • BlokMix · Blok MixNo inner capital, no space.
  • blokmixLowercase is for the logo, not for text.

A2

The block is the logo.

The wordmark swaps the o for a pink block. The app icon shows that block grabbed and lifted out of its slot on a dotted canvas, rendered in 3D. Wherever the brand appears, that one pink block is the thing people remember.

The final vector files and construction rules are on the logo masters sheet. Use those files, not the previews on this page.

blkmix
Clear space
The width of the pink block in the wordmark, on all four sides.
Minimum size
Wordmark 80 px wide on screen, 20 mm in print. App icon down to 64 px from the render; below that use the flat icon, and the pixel-snapped favicons at 32 and 16 px.
Backgrounds
Ink, concrete or paper with a pink block. On hot pink, the block turns ink. Never pink on pink.
Motion
Only the pink block moves. The letters and grid stay still.
Shadow alternate
The o becomes a square ring and a hot pink shadow sits about 10% of the letter height down and to the right. Use it for posters, merch and big moments. The block wordmark stays the primary logo. Never put the shadow version on pink.

A3

Pink is the drop, not the wall.

Ink and paper carry most of every layout. Hot pink is reserved for the thing that matters: the block, the action, the moment. The core pink is the CSS keyword hotpink, so the brand color is something any web designer can type.

A4

One family, stretched wide.

Archivo is a variable typeface with a width axis. Pushed to its widest and heaviest, it becomes the flyer voice of the wordmark and headlines. At normal width it sets clean, readable body text. JetBrains Mono handles labels, specs and liner notes. All three are free on Google Fonts.

B1

Canvas, slot and the lift.

The same world as the app icon: a dotted design canvas, a slot carved into it, and a glossy pink block lifted out with real light and shadow. Use these in place of stock photos or gradients.

The canvas and the slot

A paper canvas with dots every 64 px at icon scale, and slots carved into it where a block came from or will land. Use them as backgrounds, empty states and drop targets.

The lift

The hero object: one glossy pink block, rotated about −9°, tilted toward a top-left key light, with a soft shadow below and to the right. Render it in Blender from source/blokmix_blender.py for big moments.

Sections on the board

Website sections as white cards on a pegboard, with the sampled one lifted off in pink. Use this scene for feature illustrations and onboarding.

Motion: blocks lift and drop in 240 ms with a small overshoot, easing cubic-bezier(.3, 1.4, .5, 1). Only the pink block moves.

B2

Talk like a producer showing a friend their crate.

Blokmix borrows hip-hop’s craft words (sample, flip, crate, drop) because they describe what the tool does. It doesn’t borrow slang or put on a voice. Respect for the culture is part of the brand.

Short and on beat

Short sentences with rhythm. Cut every word the beat doesn’t need.

Show the source

Name the site a block came from. Credit is a feature.

Confident, never cocky

Say what it does plainly. Let the work make the noise.

Inspired by, not imitating

Use craft words, not slang. No “yo”, no “fr”, no fire emoji.

B3

Product words

Use these names everywhere: product UI, docs and marketing. Plain words win over craft words in the product: a person who doesn’t design should never have to learn a term. Craft words (dig, drop, flip) stay fine in marketing talk.

Section= a band of the page

Hero, features, pricing, footer. The unit of everything.

Block= a piece of a section

A heading, a button, a card, a picture. What you select and restyle.

Layout= a section’s arrangement

Built in, wireframe-plain, ready to take your style. Step through them with the arrows.

Clip= capture a section

Take a section you like from a live site, structure and all.

Crate= where your clips live

Every layout and clip, tagged by type and source.

Theme= a whole look

Colors, type, shapes and block looks over the same layouts, in one click.

Your style= your look on everything

Your theme, brand color and words on every section. “Original” shows a section as it came.

Mix= your site in progress

Pages of sections under one style. Save versions, start from any of them.

Credits= where it came from

The site a layout or clip came from, shown in its Source tab and the export.

Export= hand it to your AI

The files and a prompt to paste, for Astro or Next.js.

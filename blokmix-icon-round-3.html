03 · Shortlist

The three I’d take forward

Each shown next to the wordmark you love, since the icon has to sit beside it.

Mixed cube

The most iconic shape of the eight, and it holds up perfectly at 16 px. The three faces tell the mix story. It also leaves a lot of room for motion: faces sliding in from different directions and snapping together.

The sample

The strongest story. It explains what Blokmix does to anyone who knows sampling, and its pink tile stands out on a home screen. The record’s rings get lost at 16 px, so the favicon would keep just the disc and the lifted square.

Dither

The most web-native. The mix is drawn with pixels, which no music app would do, so it is very ownable. Good at every size, and the pixel edge echoes the square o in the shadow wordmark.

Ratings are my read of how clearly each concept says “block”, how clearly it says “mix”, and how well it holds at 16 px. Pick one, or a pair to combine, such as the cube with dithered faces, and I’ll build it as final masters and replace the 2×2 everywhere.
